# Lore

The story of the game's world, written as prose for the people who make it.
The narrator never reads these pages. It reads
[`data/story/lorebook.json`](../../data/story/lorebook.json), a set of short
entries drawn from them, and only the entries the player has earned
([world-lore](../mechanics/world-lore.md)). These pages hold the full story,
secrets included; a lorebook entry condenses a paragraph here.

When the story changes, change it here first, then the entries that carry it.
A new NPC topic, beat or lorebook entry should trace back to a paragraph on
one of these pages.

- **[The city](calder.md):** Calder, the river and its bridges, the port, the
  hospital, the stadium, the people in charge.
- **[Ashby](ashby.md):** the player's neighbourhood: its past, the building,
  the shops and who ran them.
- **[People](people.md):** the neighbours the player can meet, and the people
  they talk about.
- **[The truth](the-truth.md):** what really started it, who knew, and how the
  player can find out. Spoilers.
- **[Timeline](timeline.md):** the days before the game and the days of it,
  as the world sees them.
