# Timeline

The game starts on day 1 at 08:00. The stage times are the beats in
[world-lore](../mechanics/world-lore.md); everything before day 1 is
backstory the player learns, never sees.

## Before the game

- **Last winter:** Velloran Biologics starts funding the Ashby Community
  Clinic.
- **Summer:** the clinic starts Velloran's paid VX-7 study. Emma designs the
  recruitment flyers; Danny, Tomas Novak and a few dozen others from Ashby sign
  up. The vacant flat's last tenant leaves one night.
- **Late September:** the first volunteers run fevers. The *Merrow Star* is held
  at anchor off the terminal with a sick crew, and the news films her daily.
- **A week before the sirens:** Tomas Novak's fever won't break; his wife
  drives him to St. Agnes and they don't come back. Dr. Imrie calls Velloran;
  Velloran's memo tells the clinic to stop enrolling and tell no one.
- **The days before day 1:** fever patients pile up at St. Agnes and some bite
  the staff. The news names it the "Ashby fever". The mayor calls it a bad flu
  season. Crowds strip the corner store and the pharmacy; Mr. Haddad, Mrs. Pell
  and Walt Dunmore shut up and leave; the FreshWay locks its doors. The player
  has lived in Ashby a few weeks.
- **Day 1, two days before the sirens:** Danny stops answering his phone.

## The game

| When | What the world sees |
| --- | --- |
| Day 1, 08:00 | The game starts. The news is all fever. |
| Day 3, 06:00 | **The sirens.** The emergency broadcast: stay indoors, keep away from the feverish. The police stop answering. The dead walk. The Blue Line stops; the phones jam; the mayor goes silent. |
| Day 4 | Smoke over downtown near St. Agnes, and it doesn't stop. |
| Day 4, 07:00 | **The evacuation.** The radio names Calder Stadium; buses load at Tanner Park; soldiers wave cars across the bridges. That night, Dock Road's windows go in. |
| Day 5 | The TV goes to test cards; the internet dies. Texts from the stadium: queues, tents, soldiers checking arms. |
| Day 6, 05:00 | **The cordon.** The army closes the bridges. Helicopters over the river; the east bank loses its signal; the stadium goes quiet. |
| Day 9 | The Ferry Street Bridge burns in the middle. |
| Day 9, 12:00 | **The loop.** The radio's live voice stops mid-sentence; a recorded message takes over. |
| Day 11 | The last helicopter. |
| Day 14, 19:00 | **The power goes,** across the city, all at once. Ashby is black at night; the stadium's floodlights go out. |
| Day 21, 10:00 | **The water stops.** Only roof tanks are left. |
