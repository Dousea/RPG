# People

## The neighbours the player can meet

### Emma

Emma Hale, 28, lives next door in 3A. She grew up in a small town upriver and
came to Calder for art school, stayed for the work, and moved to Ashby for the
rent. She is a freelance graphic designer: menus, gig posters, and whatever
corporate work pays the bills. She is outgoing and generous and the first
neighbour to knock on the player's door.

Her younger brother **Danny**, 22, followed her to the city and never quite
landed: warehouse shifts, a broken lease, her sofa for a month. In September,
Emma did a rush job for a client called Velloran: recruitment flyers for a
paid study at the Ashby Community Clinic. "Paid volunteers wanted. Earn while
you heal." Danny saw the proofs on her desk and signed up for the rent money.
He stopped answering his phone two days before the sirens.

Emma tells the player about Danny once she trusts them (`emma_brother`). Only
later, and only to someone she trusts more, does she admit the flyers were
hers (`emma_flyers`). She has not forgiven herself, and she does not yet know
that Danny was among the first to start the drug; the binder says so.

### Marcus

Marcus, 52, is the building's super. He worked the container terminal for
twenty years until the automation, then took this job for the flat and the
quiet. He is practical, reliable and slow to trust; he carries a toolbox
everywhere and knows every pipe in the building.

What he knows, and tells only when asked:

- **The neighbours** (`marcus_tenants`): the Novaks down the hall left in a
  hurry the week before the sirens, because Tomas had a fever that wouldn't
  break and his wife drove him to St. Agnes. They never came back.
- **The port** (`marcus_port`): a friend still at the terminal told him the
  *Merrow Star*'s crew had a common flu and not one of them has turned. Marcus
  never believed the ship story, and this is why.
- **The roof tank** (`roof_tank`): once the bridges close and he trusts the
  player, he tells them the roof tank holds the building's water after the
  mains go, if someone opens the valve in the boiler room. He has the key.

### Jen

Jen, 23, is the barista at Halfmoon Coffee. She is bubbly, nosy and
observant; she knows every regular's name and order, and most of their
business. She is studying nursing part-time, because her mother is a nurse.

What she knows:

- **The news** (`outbreak_news`): what the news calls it, and that the ones
  who die of it get back up. She believes the ship story, like everyone.
- **The clinic's regulars** (`clinic_regulars`): the people from the clinic's
  paid study came in for coffee on payday, a bandage in the crook of every
  arm. Lately they had looked grey, and one fell asleep at the counter. This
  is gossip before the sirens, and the first hint in the game.
- **Her mother** (`jen_mother`): her mother is a nurse at St. Agnes. Her last
  texts said the first fever patients all had the same bandage in the crook
  of the arm, and that staff were told to say nothing. Then the texts
  stopped.

## People the player hears about

- **Danny Hale**, Emma's brother: a study volunteer, missing since two days
  before the sirens. His name is near the top of the binder's list.
- **Tomas Novak** and his wife, from down the hall. Tomas, a retired bus
  driver with bad nerves in his hands, took the study for the money and the
  promise of the cure. He is on the binder's list too; his fever chart starts
  a week before the sirens.
- **Dr. Ruth Imrie**, who runs the Ashby Community Clinic. Sixty-one, tired,
  and trusted by everyone who can't afford a doctor downtown. Velloran's money
  kept her clinic open, and she signed it up for the study. When the volunteers
  started running fevers she called Velloran, and Velloran told her to stop
  enrolling and say nothing. The last pages in the binder are her own
  temperatures, written in a shaking hand.
- **Mr. Haddad** of the corner store, **Mrs. Pell** of the pharmacy and **Walt
  Dunmore** of the hardware store: shopkeepers who shut up and left before the
  sirens ([Ashby](ashby.md)).
- **Jen's mother**, a nurse at St. Agnes, last heard from on the day of the
  sirens.
- **Mayor Helen Strand**, the colonel who replaced her voice on the radio, and
  the soldiers on the bridges ([The city](calder.md)).
